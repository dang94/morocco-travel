The Ultimate Moroccan Packing List

From the bustling heat of the medinas to the freezing nights of the High Atlas, versatility is your greatest asset.

Clothing

Breathable Linens

In the winding medinas of Fes or Marrakech, airflow is essential. Pack lightweight linen shirts and wide-leg trousers to remain modest while staying cool under the African sun.

Atlas Layers

The mountains demand respect. Even in summer, nights at high altitude drop significantly. A packable down jacket or a heavy merino wool layer is non-negotiable for trekkers.

Sun Protection Modest Attire Moisture Wicking

Footwear

The uneven cobblestones of the souks and the dusty trails of the Sahara require versatile shoes. A pair of broken-in hiking boots and stylish, supportive sandals are essential.
